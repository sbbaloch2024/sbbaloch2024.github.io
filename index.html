About Me

Hi! I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Iowa, advised by Dr. Bijaya Adhikari. I work on AI for Health and general machine learning.

Previously, I earned my bachelor's degree in Computer Science from DePauw University in Indiana, where I was advised by Dr. Brian Howard and Dr. Dee Wu on deep learning for clinical systems and generative models. I have also worked as an undergraduate research research assistant under Dr. Gloria Townsend.

My research interests broadly span artificial intelligence for healthcare, applied machine learning, deep learning, natural language processing. I am especially interested in building large-scale deep learning pipelines that improve clinical systems and assist in clinical decision support.

I have a deep passion for learning mathematics and modeling clinical problems using mathematical constructs. In my free time, I enjoy learning about famous mathematicians, with Srinivasa Ramanujan being my favorite.
